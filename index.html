How it Works

Arbitrage betting (or "Sure Betting") is a simple mathematical trick. By comparing odds from different bookmakers, we can find situations where the total probability of all outcomes is less than 100%.

1

We calculate the Implied Probability for each outcome (1 divided by the Odds).

2

If the sum of all probabilities is less than 100%, a guaranteed profit exists.

3

The tool calculates exactly how much money to put on each result so you win the same amount no matter who wins the match.
